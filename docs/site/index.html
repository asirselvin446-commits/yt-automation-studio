YT Automation Studio

A personal desktop tool for automatically preparing and uploading videos to your own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Watches a folder you choose for new videos.
  • Transcribes each video and generates a title, description, and tags.
  • Uploads the video to your own YouTube channel automatically.

Your data

The app accesses your YouTube account only to upload your own videos and read your channel info. Access tokens are encrypted and stored in your own private database. See the Privacy Policy for details.
